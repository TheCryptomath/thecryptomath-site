How Score Scan works

Each scan evaluates a universe of top-market-cap crypto assets and scores every one of them twice, once Long, once Short. The score aggregates multiple families of evidence: macro context, liquidity and flows, valuation, momentum, market structure, volatility, volume, sentiment and derivatives positioning, weighted for the current market regime, Bull, Range or Bear.

A detection is published only when confluence clears the bar for that regime, and the direction with the stronger case wins. Data availability is measured on every scan, and partial-data conditions are flagged.

Behind the published layer, Score Scan runs a research layer. Alternative scoring hypotheses are evaluated in shadow on the same market data, tracked prospectively, and compared against the live system before anything changes. What is published stays stable; what is being tested stays private until it earns its place.

None of this makes the score a probability of success. It makes it measurable. That is the point of this page.

Methodology and limits

The score measures confluence across Macro, Technical and Psychology inputs. It is experimental and does not represent a historical probability of success. Legacy Signal and Watchlist tiers are merged publicly as Experimental detection because their historical hierarchy has not demonstrated reliable performance separation.

A detection is marked Active during its first 24 hours, then remains visible in extended tracking until the backend window ends. The +7d milestone is kept for research and transparency, not as a recommended holding period.

Prices are checked once per hour. Milestone columns capture the first observation after the target horizon, not the exact price at the precise minute. MFE and MAE are the best and worst directional moves seen during hourly checks, not tick-by-tick extremes.

The scanner does not provide entries, stops, take-profits or expected returns. What you see is what happened to price after each published detection. Nothing more, nothing less.
