Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

What this site does

thecryptomath.com is a mostly static website. It does not require an account and does not ask you to provide personal information to browse pages.

The Cryptomath Score

Some pages provide tools and forms, such as the Cryptomath Score at /score/. When you use these tools, the service may process the inputs you submit to generate results.

We do not ask for your name. You do not need an account to use the tool.

Cloudflare may process basic technical data (such as IP address and user agent) to deliver and protect the service.

Data retention

The tool is designed not to store the content of your Score inputs on the website. Standard server logs may exist for security and reliability purposes.

Score Scan access data

Score Scan access requests use a Google Form. The information below describes the current practices confirmed by TheCryptomath, the service administrator. It concerns private access requests, separately from inputs entered in the free Score tool.

  • Telegram username (required). Identifies the account whose private access must be activated and matches it to the crypto payment received.
  • Wallet used for payment (required). Used to find and manually verify the crypto transfer associated with the access request.
  • Payment network (optional). Helps locate the transfer on the network used for payment.
  • Transaction hash or explorer link (optional). Supporting information used only when the payment is difficult to find or match manually.
  • Cryptomasks / Zodiacmasks holder status (required answer, including “No”). Used only to determine eligibility for the extra months available to holders under the current offer.
  • Discord username (optional). Can help verify holder status through Collab.Land when that status is already visible on Discord. It is not required for all holders.
  • Holder wallet (conditional). Needed only if you claim the holder benefit and your status has not already been verified through Discord / Collab.Land. If that verification has already been completed, the holder wallet is not needed.
  • Email address. No email address is collected by the Score Scan access form. It is not needed to match your Telegram username to your payment or activate access.

Storage, access and use

According to the administrator, responses are stored only in Google Forms, with no linked Google Sheet or other database used for these responses. TheCryptomath is the sole service administrator and the only person who consults them under current practices. They are used to manually verify and match the payment, activate the corresponding private access, and check eligibility for holder benefits when claimed. These statements describe the service’s practices, not all processing carried out by Google.

Retention and requests

No fixed retention period is currently defined for Google Forms responses. To request correction or deletion of your responses, contact thecryptomath@proton.me.
